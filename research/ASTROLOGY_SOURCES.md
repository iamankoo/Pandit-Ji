# Research — Astrology Sources

Separate:
1. calculation definitions
2. classical/traditional rules
3. school-specific methodologies
4. modern commentary
5. empirical/backtesting evidence
6. product explanations

Source registry fields:
title, author/tradition, edition, language, copyright/license, system, extracted rules, citation location, authority/confidence class.

Research families:
Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra traditions, Phaladeepika, Brihat Jataka, Jataka Parijata, Saravali, KP literature, Lal Kitab, Nadi, Panchang/Muhurta, Western astrology, palmistry.

Traditional texts establish what a tradition teaches; they do not by themselves establish scientific validity of prediction.

Check copyright and translation rights before ingestion/training.
