# Research — Astrology Sources

This file is the single canonical source registry for Pandit Ji. Do not create a competing registry elsewhere (`SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`). Every source used to justify an astrology rule must have an entry here, with an honest research status.

Separate:
1. calculation definitions
2. classical/traditional rules
3. school-specific methodologies
4. modern commentary
5. empirical/backtesting evidence
6. product explanations

Research families:
Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra traditions, Phaladeepika, Brihat Jataka, Jataka Parijata, Saravali, Uttara Kalamrita, Jaimini, KP literature, Lal Kitab, Nadi, Panchang/Muhurta, Western astrology, numerology, palmistry/Samudrika, and traditional remedies (puja, paddhati, vrata, mantra, yantra).

Traditional texts establish what a tradition teaches; they do not by themselves establish scientific validity of prediction.

Check copyright and translation rights before ingestion/training. Community uploads (for example on archive.org) are recorded as locations only; text is not ingested or redistributed without a valid legal basis (`LEGAL_REGULATIONS.md`).

## 1. Vocabularies

**Source classification**: `PRIMARY_CLASSICAL`, `CLASSICAL_COMMENTARY`, `TRADITIONAL_SECONDARY`, `MODERN_REFERENCE`, `ACADEMIC`, `MODERN_POPULAR`, `UNVERIFIED`, `CONTESTED`. A source may carry more than one (for example a translation of a primary text with translator notes is `PRIMARY_CLASSICAL` for the verses and `CLASSICAL_COMMENTARY` for the notes).

**Research status**: `NOT_RESEARCHED`, `IDENTIFIED`, `PARTIALLY_REVIEWED`, `FULLY_REVIEWED`, `SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `METHODOLOGY_LOCKED`.
`FULLY_REVIEWED` is used only when the full coverage defined for that source was actually completed. Opening a chapter or reading a search result never justifies it.

**Verification level** (recorded per rule and per source):
- `OCR-TRANSLATION`: English read in an OCR text of a scan.
- `IMAGE-TRANSLATION`: English checked against the page image.
- `PRINT-TRANSLATION`: English checked against the physical book.
- `OCR-SOURCE-LANGUAGE (unreviewed)`: Sanskrit/Hindi read in OCR by a non-qualified reader. Useful evidence, never a verification.
- `SANSKRIT-LEVEL`: Sanskrit reviewed by a qualified reviewer. Never claimed otherwise.

"TRANSLATION-LEVEL VERIFIED" means one of the first three levels. "SANSKRIT-LEVEL VERIFIED" means the last level only.

**Verse versus note**: every citation states whether the passage is a `verse` or a translator/commentator `note`. A note is never cited as if it were a verse.

**Absence claims**: an OCR keyword search is only a discovery probe. A term or rule that is not found in OCR is recorded as `NOT_VERIFIED_BY_OCR`, never as `ABSENT_FROM_SOURCE`. A source is only recorded as lacking a rule after the relevant chapters were actually read.

## 2. Entry schema

`id`, `title`, `author`, `tradition/school`, `original language`, `translation (translator, language)`, `edition`, `publisher`, `publication year`, `volume`, `chapter coverage`, `subject coverage`, `source classification`, `authority level`, `directly verified (how)`, `verification level`, `sanskrit status`, `full text examined`, `source_kind coverage (verse/note)`, `relevant Pandit Ji phases`, `relevant modules`, `research status`, `rules extracted (rule and location)`, `conflicting interpretations found`, `copyright/licensing`, `public location`, `citation format`, `translation quality`, `OCR reliability`, `canonical implementation eligibility`.
