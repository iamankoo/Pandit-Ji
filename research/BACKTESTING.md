# Research — Evaluation & Backtesting

Layers:
1. Calculation correctness
2. Rule application correctness
3. Interpretation fidelity
4. Historical prediction performance where suitable labels exist

Store anonymized case ID, birth inputs, calculation configuration, target event/date, pre-event evidence, prediction and outcome.

Avoid future-information leakage.

Metrics may include accuracy, precision/recall, F1, calibration, Brier score, MAE for time estimates, coverage and abstention rate.

Compare against simple baselines and suitable non-astrological reference models.

Never market a small internal result as proof of astrology. Report sample size, method, uncertainty and limitations.
