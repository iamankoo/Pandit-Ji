# Research — Competitors

## AstroSage
Public pages show a broad paid ecosystem: instant reports, question services, detailed reports, cloud chart storage, astrologer/counselling services and specialized reports. Current public examples include prices around ₹299, ₹399, ₹499 and ₹996 for selected services.

## KundliGPT
Public pages show AI astrology chat, chart-aware answers, Vedic + Western astrology, Dasha, Panchang, matching, premium reports and multilingual support. Current pricing uses coins/pay-as-you-go plus a 1-day unlimited chat pass and states there is no recurring subscription.
